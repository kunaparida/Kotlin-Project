Claims Management System - Walkthrough Script (20 Minutes)

1. Introduction (1 Minute)
What to Say

"I built a Claims Management System using Java 21 and Spring Boot 3 following a modular monolith architecture. The system supports three roles: Claimant, Officer, and Manager. Claimants submit claims, officers process them through a controlled workflow, and managers monitor workload, liability, and team performance."

2. Business Problem (1 Minute)
What to Say

"Insurance companies need a system to manage claim lifecycles from submission to settlement. The application enforces business rules, tracks audit history, and provides dashboard insights while ensuring secure role-based access."

"Every status change creates an audit record and an outbox event in the same database transaction. This avoids the dual-write problem."

Why Outbox?

Without outbox:

Plain Text
DB Updated ✅
Kafka Failed ❌

Data inconsistency.

With outbox:

Plain Text
Everything committed together ✅
Kafka Question

If asked:

"Did you actually use Kafka?"

Answer:

"Kafka support is implemented and unit-tested, but default execution uses LoggingEventPublisher. Switching app.events.publisher=kafka and providing bootstrap servers enables Kafka publishing without changing business logic."

Panel Q&A (10-15 Minutes)

Be ready for:

Why Monolith?

Single bounded context, transactional consistency, faster delivery. Future split: Notifications and Reporting.

Why JWT?

Stateless authentication suitable for REST APIs.

Why Outbox?

Prevents lost events and dual-write issues.

Why Not Direct Kafka?

Database and Kafka can become inconsistent. Outbox solves that.

Why H2?

Development convenience. PostgreSQL configuration already prepared.

Why Claim History?

Audit trail and compliance requirements.
